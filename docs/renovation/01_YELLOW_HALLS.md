- **Prototype.** The corridor, junction and bay read as one architecture. Routes have two walking columns and two blocks of headroom. Materials and signs read clearly, lit and low-power cases remain navigable, segments align, and every prop has a plausible support. Check the archived sign views at three and six blocks and the low-power route target of at least four block light. Record how light was measured using a supported capability.
- **Generated dimension.** Recheck two repeated turns, all eight bays, reserved empty volume, fixture/sign variants, portal connectivity, and same-seed repeatability through rebuilds. Walk the arrival, normal escape-button return and the Library pad travel. Verify functional hazards and signs retain their behavior and all required paths remain accessible. Capture screenshots from the generated route on the tested build. Complete the evidence and regression gates in the [tracker](../IMPLEMENTATION_TODO.md#acceptance-evidence).
- **Placement risks.** The current segment helper counts successful writes even when others fail. Check which callers use the result and reproduce partial assembly, rotated footprints, unloaded chunks and late reservation collisions. Verify structure results and actual occupied cells. Review structure masks with Z-fastest NBT indexing, preserving untouched cells and functional payloads. Check native lights or later passes for fixture and sign overwrite.
- **Lifecycle risks.** The existing patch job awaits its module and refreshes pad reservations before special sites, while individual decor writes may still fail silently. Reproduce failure/retry, forced rebuild, stale jobs, multiple owners, disconnects, late pad relocation and ticking-area cleanup in live QA. Check that required placements complete before the generated marker, and measure tick/queue behavior against the existing workload budgets. Start with the existing Yellow Halls decor, module, pad-reservation, lifecycle and patch-job tests and the module-mask validator tests.
